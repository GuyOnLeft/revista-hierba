How many pages are we building?

The nav has 4 links. The question is whether sections also get their own dedicated pages.

A

4 Pages Only

/editorial — The magazine's editorial voice
/secciones — One page showing all 4 topics with article teasers
/nosotros — About the publication
/contacto — Contact form

Clean scope. Secciones page shows Cannabis, Plantas, Ciencia, DDHH in a grid — no sub-pages per topic.

B

4 Nav Pages + 4 Section Sub-Pages

/editorial, /nosotros, /contacto — same as above
/secciones — index linking to each topic
/secciones/cannabis — full article list for Cannabis
/secciones/plantas — Plantas Medicinales articles
/secciones/ciencia — Ciencia articles
/secciones/derechos — Derechos Humanos articles

More pages, but each section has a proper home with room for future articles.

C

4 Nav Pages + 4 Section Sub-Pages + Article Template

Everything in Option B, plus an /articulo/[slug] article detail page template — so article cards on the home page and section pages are actually clickable and go somewhere.

Fullest prototype. Makes the site feel like a real magazine with working article links.
